Notes from a fictional classroom study

Retrieval practice means trying to recall information before checking the source. In this study, students closed their notes and wrote what they remembered.

Spaced practice separates study sessions over time. Here, the sessions took place on Monday and Thursday.

Feedback tells learners what they recalled incorrectly. After each recall attempt, these students compared their answers with the lesson notes.

Transfer means applying what was learned to a different task. The transfer task in this study asked students to use a taught rule on a new diagram.

Students who used retrieval practice scored 12 percentage points higher on the final quiz than the comparison group. The study included only 24 students from one class. The author says this result does not establish that retrieval practice caused the difference, because the students chose their own groups.
